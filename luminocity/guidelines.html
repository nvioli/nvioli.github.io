Community Guidelines

Last updated: November 20, 2026

LuminoCity works because people check in honestly and treat each other decently. These guidelines explain what we expect, and what happens if that breaks down.

Check in for real

Ratings should reflect the venue you're actually at, right now. Fake, exaggerated, or bulk check-ins (your own or someone else's venue) distort the map for everyone and will be removed.

Be someone you'd want to run into

No harassment, threats, hate speech, or targeted abuse. No sexually explicit content. No impersonating another person or venue. This applies to profiles, check-ins, feed posts, and photos alike.

Respect other people's privacy

Don't post identifying photos or information about someone else without their consent, and don't use check-ins to track or out another person's location.

Reporting and blocking

Every profile, check-in, and feed post in the app has a Report action. Blocking someone immediately hides your activity from each other and removes any friend connection between you. Reports go to us for review — they're not visible to the person reported.

What happens when guidelines are broken

Depending on severity and whether it's a first occurrence, we may:

  • Remove the specific content
  • Send a warning
  • Temporarily restrict an account
  • Permanently suspend an account, for severe or repeated violations

If you think a decision about your account was a mistake, email nvioli@gmail.com and we'll take a second look.

These guidelines work alongside our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
