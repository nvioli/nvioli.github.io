Support

We usually reply within a couple of days.

Something broken, confusing, or missing? Email nvioli@gmail.com and we'll help. A few common questions below first, in case they save you a round trip.

I didn't get my sign-in email

LuminoCity uses passwordless sign-in: you enter your email and get a one-time link, no password to forget. Check spam/junk first. If it's genuinely not arriving, email us the address you signed up with and we'll look into it.

A venue is missing from the map, or shouldn't be there

Use "Report" on the venue's page in the app, or email us the venue name and address.

Someone is harassing me or posting something inappropriate

Block and Report are available from any user's profile and from individual posts/check-ins in the app. See our Community Guidelines for what we do and don't allow, and how reports are handled.

I want to delete my account or my data

See the account deletion page — you can do this yourself in the app, or ask us to do it for you if you no longer have access.

I have a question about my data

Read our Privacy Policy, or email us directly if it doesn't answer your question.
