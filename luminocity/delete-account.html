Delete Your Account

Two ways to do this: in the app, or by asking us.

Option 1 — In the app (immediate)

Open LuminoCity → Profile → Delete account. This is permanent and immediate. It removes:

  • Your profile (username, photo, email)
  • Every check-in you've made
  • Every feed post and photo you've posted
  • Your friend connections, both directions

There's no recovery window — once confirmed, it's gone.

Option 2 — By email (no app required)

If you no longer have the app installed, no longer have access to your sign-in email, or just prefer not to use the app, email nvioli@gmail.com from the email address your account uses and ask us to delete your account. We'll verify it's you and complete the deletion within a few days, then confirm by email.

What isn't deleted

Aggregate, de-identified data that was already folded into a venue's public energy average can't be un-mixed from that average after the fact, the same way an anonymous poll response can't be un-counted. No row or record identifying you survives account deletion.

See our Privacy Policy for more on what we collect and why.
